# The skeptic critic's assessment of round one

Round one is broad and its code-level facts hold. About twenty fresh checks confirmed the reported facts, including the loaded Saturday launchd job and the July noise figures. The 332-of-337 survival rate overstates what was tested. The truth lens refuted 1 of 293 findings but rewrote the claim in 258, and 141 survivors (25 highs) carry corrections such as 'latent', 'inferred' or 'not reproduced'. 'Confirmed' means the mechanism exists. No reviewer could run a model or see production, so every wrong-answer impact is inferred. Severity, remedies and reconciliation are the weak layers. (1) Each severity came from one verifier (medium confidence for 23 of 45 highs), and about 170 findings record that no second-opinion pass was available. Where two verifiers independently rated one defect, 13 of 20 high clusters split high and medium. The 45 highs are about 34 root causes, and about 75 findings restate another. (2) Nothing reconciled findings: fifteen clusters prescribe incompatible fixes, and four survivors recommend what decision (g) forbids. (3) Fixes were rarely executed: on the September gate run both proposed citation-label checks catch nothing in 150 labels and flag one or two correct answers. (4) Reviewers stopped at 12 findings (24 of 32 returned exactly 12): the agent has 14.8 findings per thousand lines against 3.4 in the pipeline, and 1,550 lines of coverage-frame selection have none. (5) Severity ignores sequencing. Every corpus fix needs a release, yet stored retrieval runs score 0.30 to 0.36 recall against a 0.9 floor and the September gate run scored 2.60 against 2.75; both findings saying so are medium. The fixes total about 1,370 numbered steps, many adding gates and paid evaluations to a release path that has never completed.
